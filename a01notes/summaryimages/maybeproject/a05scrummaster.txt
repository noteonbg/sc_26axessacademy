Morning (Start of the Day)

1. Check the board for blocked tasks.
2. Read team messages to catch  issues early.

Identify the main goal to focus on in today's standup.

Mid-Morning (Standup & Sync)
3. Run a strict, 15-minute Daily Standup focused on the Sprint Goal.
4. Write down every blocker and impediment raised by the team.
5. Set up quick follow-up chats for topics that need deeper discussion.

Midday (Unblocking & Shielding)
6. Contact stake holders to  clear away blockers.
7. Sync with the SME or academic leads to keep user stories clear.

Afternoon (Coaching & Meetings)
8. Check in with team members to listen to concerns and coach them.
9. Track progress on improvements decided in the last Retrospective.

End of Day (Wrap-Up)
10. Update the status of active blockers and open dependencies.
11. Note down the top priority to tackle first thing tomorrow morning.
